Export Your Data

Download all your wardrobe data — clothing items (including images), outfits, tags, and wear history — as a single JSON file. Use this as a backup or to migrate to another device.

Import Data

Import a previously exported Trousseau JSON file. All clothing items, outfits, tags, and wear history in the file will be added to your account. Existing data will not be removed.

#{dataManagementBean.importResult}

What is included in the export?

  • All clothing items with images, receipts, and wear tracking stats
  • All outfits with their clothing item associations and seasons
  • All custom tags
  • Full outfit wear history
  • Ratings, comments, and shares are not included
